# 2026-11-24_ste_l110 record

## Purpose

STE task L1.10 (main track, INFO): does a 3v3 team trained with the tag distance **drawn per game within 1.5–1.7 m** and
the nominal estimate error still play well at the fixed 1.6 m tag, with and without the error? A real tag judged on the
drones' estimates will not fire at exactly 1.6 m; this asks whether training on a spread of distances costs anything.
Spec `ste_learning/drafts/L1_9_10_SPEC.md` (written before the runs, frozen 24 Nov 2026 at `242953c5`; its pre-registered
reading words are used below, unchanged).

## Plain reading

Training with the tag drawn between 1.5 and 1.7 m and with the estimate error gave teams that are not distinguishable
from T46's at the fixed 1.6 m game (one seed each, intervals overlap), with or without the error. The pattern is T46's
and T57's too ([T57](2026-11-22_ste_l112_score_record.md): T46's Guard 913 v 986 against converge, 922 v 960 against
sacrifice): each team beats the scripts against the opponent it trained on and the ones like it, and is below them
elsewhere, narrowly for the Guard team, by far for the Strike team. The Strike team is the sharp case: 681 against the
Guard it trained on, 39 against field Guards (the best script reaches 280 there). One seed per side.

## What this does not show

Tier 1 only (no Isaac Lab drone); one trained network per side, one seed; the estimate-error values are EXPLORATORY
(T63, one hover log); the drawn tag distance is not an input to the networks; scripts as opponents, no trained
opponent; nothing has flown.
