## Confounds

- **px4_version**: PX4 v1.16.1 SITL in the AirStack simulator; tier 2 flies a port of PX4 v1.14.0's cascade (models/px4_cascade.py). Not removable here: named.
- **route**: the speed-only MAVROS route: 35 of 35 steps sent velocity (and yaw rate) only (type mask 1479). Tier 2 emulates it with its two position gains at 0; the real drone's trajectory-setpoint route (position + velocity + acceleration) is not part of this comparison.
- **clock**: the simulator's PX4 clock against the commander's wall clock, per step (fitted); removed by replaying the setpoint PX4 received on PX4's clock. Its size at the policy's level: the settled setpoint PX4 got over the teleop command, per speed.
  - {"scale_min": 2.380000000000001, "scale_median": 2.536000000000001, "scale_max": 2.568000000000001, "settled_over_teleop": {"0.5": [0.36542811147868476, 0.4371789509014078], "1.0": [0.5, 0.5017952347537944], "2.0": [0.75, 0.7500867451863167]}}
- **estimator_source**: the simulator's EKF2 fuses simulated GPS (EKF2_GPS_CTRL 7, EKF2_HGT_REF 1, EKF2_EV_CTRL 0); the drone fuses mocap. The error arm's values (T63) are the real drone_2's EKF2 against mocap, not this EKF2's error.
- **plant**: not matched by tier 2: THR_MDL_FAC 0.8999999761581421 (tier 2 linear), the hover-thrust estimator (MPC_USE_HTE 1), Pegasus's input scaling 1350 against omega_max 1450, the USD's inertia (not read; v1's values used), PhysX scene and solver settings of each simulator.
